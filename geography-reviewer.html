Visualizing Challenges & Strategies

Hover over the images to review key concepts from Topic 7.

Economic Opportunities

Cities like Shanghai offer varied employment, centralized markets, and faster trade. They attract migrants due to high-paying jobs (Pull Factor).

Informal Settlements

Rapid growth leads to housing shortages. Areas like the Favelas in Rio de Janeiro form as residents self-build homes. Managed via top-down or bottom-up schemes.

Transport Challenges

Pollution and congestion are major environmental challenges. London combats this with a Congestion Charge and Ultra Low Emission Zones (ULEZ).

Sustainable Transit (Curitiba)

Curitiba, Brazil uses an innovative Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system. Tube-shaped stops allow fast boarding, reducing car trips and emissions by 40%.

Mumbai Transport Crisis

Mumbai's suburban train network carries 7.5 million people daily. Extreme overcrowding poses severe safety risks to commuters.

Green Infrastructure

Projects like 'azoteas verdes' (green roofs) in Mexico City or Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park in Singapore improve air quality, biodiversity, and mental wellbeing.

Deep Dive Case Study

Mumbai, India

Mumbai is a rapidly growing megacity, growing by half a million people per year. 60% of migrants come from villages within Maharashtra, driven by pull factors like jobs in banking, tech, and Bollywood.

  • The Dharavi Settlement Home to 1 million people. Offers strong community and informal economy (leather, textiles), but suffers from poor sanitation.
  • Transport Solutions The monorail largely failed due to poor integration. The Mumbai Master Metro Plan aims to fix this, carrying 1.7m daily passengers.
  • Mangrove Protection Vital green spaces are being cleared for treatment plants. Mangroves act as natural filters and carbon sinks.
